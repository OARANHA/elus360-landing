## Follow-up motion direction

Sequence: lead received -> unanswered follow-up -> ELUS activates -> blue signals traverse the connections -> actions and next scheduled step become visible. One deterministic timeline, playback on visibility, replay on demand, pause when offscreen or tab hidden, final static state for reduced motion. Art, copy, forms and links are unchanged.
